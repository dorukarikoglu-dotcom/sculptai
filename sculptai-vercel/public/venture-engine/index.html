Autonomous rules

Stage 1 kill: after 80 qualified visits, checkout rate < 2.5% → stop spending.

Stage 2 kill: after 150 qualified visits, 0 purchases → kill the venture.

Promote: ≥2 purchases, purchase rate ≥1.5%, and CAC ≤45% of selling price → scale.

Reallocation: strongest venture gets 40% of the next pool, #2 gets 25%, #3 gets 15%; 20% stays in exploration so new ideas can still beat incumbents.
