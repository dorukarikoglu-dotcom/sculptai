REVERSE HOTEL MARKETPLACE

You shouldn't compare 40 hotel listings. Hotels should compete for you.

Tell the agent where, when, budget and what actually matters — room category, breakfast, cancellation, late checkout, upgrade. It negotiates directly with multiple hotels and ranks the offers by total value.

The inversion

  • You publish one verified travel intent.
  • AI selects suitable hotels.
  • AI contacts them and negotiates direct-booking value.
  • You receive a ranked shortlist instead of searching OTA pages.
  • You approve the final booking — the agent never silently commits your money.

Long-term this is not just hotels: the same intent auction can expand into other high-ticket fragmented purchases.
