The agent searches continuously, filters roles, tailors applications, follows up, prepares interviews and creates negotiation strategy. The product is not “more applications”; the outcome is higher compensation.

Agent loop

  • Build one verified career profile.
  • Continuously find roles with meaningful upside.
  • Apply only above a defined fit/pay threshold.
  • Prepare role-specific interview evidence.
  • At offer stage, recommend and execute only user-approved negotiation steps.
  • Measure verified compensation uplift against the user's baseline.

Human approval remains at sensitive steps; fabricated credentials or autonomous misrepresentation are never allowed.
